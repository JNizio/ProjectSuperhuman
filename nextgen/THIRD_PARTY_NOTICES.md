## Tabler Icons

Project Superhuman locally bundles a small subset of Tabler Icons as Android vector drawables for domain-specific Cardio pictograms. The application keeps these assets behind its own icon wrapper so they can follow Project Superhuman sizing, tint, accessibility, and visual hierarchy.
